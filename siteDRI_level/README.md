# Data Centre Energy Dataset

## Overview

This directory contains data describing the operational energy consumption and infrastructure characteristics of the QMUL data centre over a one-week monitoring period. The dataset provides facility-level energy measurements alongside key data centre operating parameters and infrastructure information that can be used to support energy analysis, carbon footprint estimation, and sustainability studies.

### 1. Data Centre Energy Dataset (pue_energy_30min.csv)

This CSV file contains facility energy measurements recorded at **30-minute intervals** over a one-week period.

The dataset includes:

* **IT Energy Consumption**

  * Estimated energy consumed by the IT equipment (compute infrastructure).
  * Calculated by subtracting the IRC (In Row Chillers) contribution provided by the Data Centre Expert monitoring tool, from the measured (In Row Chiller + IT) meter energy reading.

* **Total Energy Consumption**

  * Total energy measured at the data centre level, including both IT and supporting infrastructure.

* **Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE)**

  * Measured PUE values corresponding to each 30-minute interval.

* **Timestamps**

  * Date and time associated with each measurement.

These measurements provide a time series of the facility's operational energy profile and infrastructure efficiency throughout the monitoring period.

## 2. Data Centre Configuration Information (WP3_SMARTDRI)

This file contains static information describing the characteristics of the HPC facility used during analysis.

Information includes:

* Operational voltage
* Maximum electrical power capacity
* Idle power consumption
* Total number of compute nodes
* Other infrastructure parameters/notes relevant to energy analysis

These values provide contextual information about the operating characteristics of the data centre and may be used as reference values during modelling or interpretation of results.

## Data Centre Infrastructure Notes

## District Heating system Contribution

The facility is integrated with a district heating system that recovers waste heat generated by the HPC infrastructure. The energy contribution of the DHS is also accounted for in the total energy reading of the Data Center.

## Notes

* Measurements are recorded at 30-minute intervals.
* All timestamps correspond to the local monitoring period for the data centre.
* IT energy values are derived from measured facility data and the DCE-provided IRC values.
